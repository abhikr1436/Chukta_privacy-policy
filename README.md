# Privacy Policy for Chukta

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026

Welcome to **Chukta** ("we," "our," or "the App"), a ledger (khata) and stock app for shopkeepers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

## 1. Information You Keep in the App

### A. Your ledger and stock data stays on your phone
The names, phone numbers, amounts, notes, dates, reminders and stock items you enter, plus your name and store name, are stored **only in the App's private storage on your device**.
- We do **not** run servers and we never upload, receive or see this data.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it from the device.
- If your phone's Android backup (Google One / Google Drive backup) is turned on, Android may include the App's data in your device backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account.

### B. Backup files you create
"Back up data" saves a file to a location **you choose** (for example Downloads, Google Drive or a chat). We do not receive a copy. Keep it safe: it contains your ledger data in readable form.

### C. Messages you send
Reminders, statements and orders are sent through the apps **you choose** (phone dialer, SMS, WhatsApp or another app). The App only opens that app with the text filled in; you decide whether to send it. Those apps' own privacy policies apply to what you send.

### D. Contacts
When you tap "Pick from contacts", Android shows its contact picker and gives the App access to **only the one contact you choose**, to fill in a name and phone number. The App does not read your contact list and does not upload contacts.

## 2. Permissions Used
- **INTERNET:** to show ads, process the optional Pro purchase, and send crash reports and usage statistics (see Section 3).
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:** for collection reminders and the daily low-stock reminder you set up.
- **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED:** to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
- **USE_BIOMETRIC:** for the optional app lock, which uses your phone's own fingerprint or screen lock. Your fingerprint and PIN are handled by Android and are never available to the App.
- **Advertising ID (AD_ID):** used by Google AdMob (see below).

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App uses the following Google services, which may collect information as described in their policies:

### A. Google AdMob (ads)
Free users see a banner ad on the home screen. AdMob may collect and use:
- Advertising ID
- Device information (model, operating system, language) and approximate location derived from IP address
- Ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads). Users who buy Chukta Pro see no ads, and the ads service is not started for them.

### B. Google Firebase Analytics
Collects anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, screens used, device type, OS version, app version and an app-instance identifier, to help us understand how the App is used. It does **not** include your ledger or stock data.

### C. Google Firebase Crashlytics
If the App crashes, a crash report is sent containing technical details (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and an installation identifier). It does **not** include your ledger or stock data.

### D. Google Play Billing (Chukta Pro)
The optional one-time "Chukta Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do **not** receive your card, UPI or other payment details. Google Play tells the App only whether the purchase is owned, so that ads can be removed.

Google's privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
How Google uses data from partner apps: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 4. Data Sharing and Selling
We do **not** sell your information. Apart from the Google services listed above, the App does not share data with anyone.

## 5. Data Security
Your ledger data lives in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. You can also turn on the in-app lock so the App asks for your fingerprint or screen lock when opened. Information sent to Google services travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
- **Ledger and stock data:** stays on your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
- **Analytics and crash data:** kept by Google under Firebase's retention settings. To ask for deletion of data linked to your installation, email us (Section 9).

## 7. Children's Privacy
The App is meant for shopkeepers and business owners and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
